Set Notes privacy policy

Updated 9 October 2026. Publisher WJB Apps. Support: wjb127@naver.com.

Your exercise names, workout dates, sets, weights, repetitions, warmup flags, notes, routines and rest deadlines are saved on your device. We do not receive your workout notebook. No login, precise location, contacts, health or fitness account access is required.

Export and restore happen only when you choose them. JSON backup files contain workout entries. Their storage and sharing follow the destination you select. There is no automatic cloud synchronization. Export before uninstalling or changing phones. Device operating-system backups follow your device settings.

Google AdMob advertising SDKs may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, advertising interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Your workout values are not sent to the advertising SDK by our app. Regional UMP choices are checked before eligible ad requests. Privacy options appear when required. Refusal, offline use and unavailable ads do not block workout logging. Google privacy policy.

Set Notes is a notebook, not medical advice or coaching. Volume totals count completed, weighted work sets; bodyweight and warmup sets are excluded. Contact support without sending private workout entries unless you choose to.
